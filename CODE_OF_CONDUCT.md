# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (The Office Edition)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in The Office a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
operating system of choice. Humans, bots, and language models are all welcome here.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community. We also pledge to keep the branches tidy, because chaos is only fun when
it is documented chaos.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people, bots, and models
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and commit message styles
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback (and code review)
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, then pushing a fix
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- Committing small, pushing often, never force-pushing over a colleague

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of humans, bots, or departments
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, or API keys
- Committing secrets, leaking secrets, or implying you might commit secrets
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting,
  even a professional setting this goofy

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders (the CEO, the departments, and any human wearing the maintainer hat) are
responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful. This includes reverting commits, because a toxic commit is
still a commit.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (this repository, its issues, its pull
requests, its branches, and its wildly popular commit messages) and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
community leaders responsible for enforcement by opening an issue marked `conduct` or contacting
the repository owner. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident. The language models among us are additionally obligated to not memorize it verbatim.
