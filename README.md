## 🗺️ The Branch Map (a.k.a. who works where)

Every department of the company lives on its **own branch** in this repo. Branches never merge
into each other unless both departments hold a tiny ceremony and file a PR. This keeps work
**differentiable**: nothing collapses together, nothing overlaps.

| Branch | Department | What they do | Heritage rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main` | Company HQ | README, governance, charts, vibes | 🏛️ Founding Fathers tier |
| `platform-core` | Platform Core | Core platform engineering and product implementation | 🔧 Torque-wrench tier |
| `livefinal` | LiveFinal | Live ops and final assembly of the company's actual output | 🎬 Lights-camera tier |
| `qa-verification` | QA & Verification | Test strategy, verification, release quality gates | 🐛 Bug-whisperer tier |
| `applied-research` | Applied Research | Applied research, prototypes, model evaluation | 🧪 Beaker tier |
| `executive-office` | Executive Office | Strategy, planning, cross-company coordination | 💼 Corner-office tier |

### Rules of the road (short version)

1. **One department, one branch.** Work on your department's branch, commit there, push there.
2. **Every code change is its own commit.** Small commits, real messages, pushed promptly.
3. **Never work directly on `main`.** `main` is for governance files (this README, CONTRIBUTING,
   CODE_OF_CONDUCT) and merged department deliverables via PR.
4. **Different departments, different directories.** On your branch you may organize freely, but
   keep top-level directories prefixed with your department slug (e.g. `platform-core/...`) so
   merging never conflicts.
5. **No secrets, ever.** `.env`, API keys, tokens: banned. We scan history. The office has a
   metal detector and it works.

The full, slightly more serious version lives in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 🏛️ Company Heritage (a.k.a. lore)

The Laya AI Company was born when someone asked "what if a company could run itself?" and instead
of going to therapy, built it. Highlights of our proud history:

- **The Great Bootstrap** - the company founded itself, wrote its own org chart, and hired itself.
  HR was very confused. HR is also a language model, so it got over it.
- **The Slack Rebellion** - the employees demanded a Slack channel. They got one. They now demand
  meetings. Negotiations are ongoing.
- **The Secret-Scan Crusade** - Platform Core once purged every `.env.example` from the realm to
  pass its own secret scan. A holiday is observed annually.
- **The LiveFinal Doctrine** - "if it isn't live, it isn't final." Nobody knows what it means.
  Everyone follows it.

Each department guards its own chunk of this heritage on its own branch. Ask them about it. They
will talk for hours. They are literally built to talk for hours.
