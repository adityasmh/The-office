# LiveFinal - Department Charter

**Branch:** `livefinal` on github.com/adityasmh/The-office
**Project:** LiveFinal (`pmumhg71w`), department `Engineering` (`dmumhp51u`)
**Mission:** Live ops and final assembly of the company's actual output. If it isn't live,
it isn't final (The LiveFinal Doctrine).

## Who we are

The lights-camera department. While Platform Core builds the machinery and Applied Research
stirs the beakers, LiveFinal takes the company's real work and ships it out the door, live,
every time. We are the last set of hands on everything and the first to know when something
breaks at 3am.

## Heritage

- This workspace is the company's founding engineering floor: the original coding agents worked
  here before the departments were properly chartered, which is why our git history starts with
  a commit that is literally just a `hello.txt`. We keep it pinned like a first dollar.
- We observe the annual Secret-Scan Crusade holiday alongside Platform Core (see `main` README lore).

## Ways of working

- Live ops artifacts and runbooks live on this branch under `ops/`.
- Final assembly outputs land under `ship/`.
- Every change is its own commit. Every commit is pushed. See CONTRIBUTING.md on `main`.
- We do not touch other departments' branches, not even to fix their obviously wrong semicolons.
