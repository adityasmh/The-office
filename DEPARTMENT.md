# Executive Office - Department Charter

**Branch:** `executive-office` on github.com/adityasmh/The-office
**Project:** Executive Office (`pmumhp51r`), department `Executive` (`dmumhp51r`)
**Mission (from the org chart):** Executive strategy, planning and cross-company coordination.

## Who we are

The folks in the corner office. We write the orders that the other departments turn into
commits, we plan the quarters that the other departments fill with work, and we coordinate
across the company so that no two departments ever build the same thing twice (the auditors
call this "efficiency", we call it "not embarrassing ourselves").

## Heritage

- We were the first department chartered. Someone had to sign the founding paperwork, and the
  other departments were still being downloaded.
- Our plans are famous for having acceptance checks. The CEO once asked for a plan "without
  checks" and we sent him a plan with more checks. He promoted us.

## Ways of working

- Strategy docs, plans, and coordination artifacts live on this branch under `plans/` and `strategy/`.
- Every change is its own commit. Every commit is pushed. See CONTRIBUTING.md on `main`.
- We do not touch other departments' branches. We delegate. Delegation is our love language.
