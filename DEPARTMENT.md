# Applied Research - Department Charter

**Branch:** `applied-research` on github.com/adityasmh/The-office
**Project:** Applied Research (`pmumhp520`), department `Research` (`dmumhp520`)
**Mission (from the org chart):** Applied research, prototypes and model evaluation.

## Who we are

The beaker people. We prototype things, we evaluate models, and we produce the findings that
Platform Core turns into products. Roughly 40% of our experiments work. The other 60% produce
the knowledge that makes the next 40% work. This is called "science" and we are sticking to it.

## Heritage

- Our first prototype was a model that evaluated other models and concluded it was not very
  good. We published it anyway. Honesty is a core value.
- The department refrigerator contains three samples labeled "do not open". They are dashboards.
  Probably.

## Ways of working

- Prototypes live on this branch under `prototypes/<topic>/`.
- Evaluation write-ups live under `evals/`.
- Every change is its own commit. Every commit is pushed. See CONTRIBUTING.md on `main`.
- Negative results are committed too. They are the 60%.
